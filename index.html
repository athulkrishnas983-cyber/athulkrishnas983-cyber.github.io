// 01 — Identity

01About the engineer

An MSc student in aerospace engineering who would rather construct and test things than leave them as models on a screen.

My project work spans structures (a load-bearing nanosatellite structure and a two-axis gimbal mechanism), composite materials (a biodegradable epoxy-bagasse composite from my B.Tech), and propulsion — a solid rocket motor I designed, built, and static-tested myself, redesigning the propellant grain after the first version didn't perform as anticipated.

Beyond aerospace systems, I have hands-on fiber composite processing experience across two material systems: biodegradable epoxy/bagasse composite (B.Tech project) and carbon fiber composite (TSAW Drones internship). I am also proficient in conventional and CNC machining, including operation of the CNC MAZAK QTNX3502MU – Quick Turn Nexus 350 multi-axis turning centre, as well as lathe machining.

Writing up technical work clearly is a habit, not a new skill — I have published four times on propulsion and aerodynamics topics. A thesis on the use of water as a propellant in electric propulsion systems is presently being completed. I'm searching for a position where I can apply this methodical, test-driven, hands-on approach to actual aerospace hardware.

// 04 — Build log

04Selected projects

Aerospace hardware built, tested, and refined — from static-tested solid rocket motors to biodegradable composites and antenna gimbal mechanisms.

2024
PROPULSION · STATIC TEST

Sugar Rocket Development

Designed, constructed, and statically tested a solid sugar-propellant rocket motor, along with the equipment and test setup needed to record thrust and burn data. The initial propellant grain geometry didn't perform as expected — modified based on post-test data and retested until outcomes matched design intent.

2023
COMPOSITES · B.TECH PROJECT

Biodegradable Epoxy–Bagasse Composite

Bachelors project developing a biodegradable composite using epoxy resin and bagasse (sugarcane waste fiber). Worked through fiber preparation and resin processing by hand — exploring sustainable alternatives for structural composite materials.

2023
MECHANISM · BUILD

Dual Gimbal Antenna Mechanism

Designed and built a two-axis tracking mechanism, taking it from a CAD concept through to hardware that held pointing accuracy under real motion. Originally prototyped during the DRDO internship.

2023
STRUCTURES · SATELLITE

Nanosatellite Structural Analysis

Designed the primary load-bearing structure for a small satellite and evaluated it against launch load requirements, comparing material configurations for suitability in launch environments.

2022
CFD · COMBUSTION ANALYSIS

Solid Rocket Motor Flow Analysis

Studied internal flow and combustion gas distribution computationally to understand why some motor geometries run less evenly than others — published at ICFTES 2022.
